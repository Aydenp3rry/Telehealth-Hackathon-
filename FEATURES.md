# Feature Documentation

## Patient Experience

### Guided Intake
Collects structured information before provider review.

### Body Map
Allows the patient to identify the location of a symptom or pain.

### Pain Scale
Captures reported pain severity on a 1–10 scale.

### Medications and Allergies
Prompts the patient to provide important context that may affect care.

### Multilingual Experience
The concept includes English and Spanish support.

## Provider Experience

### Patient Summary
Organizes submitted patient information into a clearer format for provider review.

### Provider Dashboard
Provides a central place for reviewing asynchronous patient submissions.

### Prioritization / Triage Concept
Uses the submitted information to help identify cases that may need faster review.

### Escalation
Higher-risk cases can move outside the standard asynchronous workflow.

### Secure Messaging Concept
Allows follow-up communication between the patient and provider.

## Extended Concept

### Remote Monitoring
The project explored incorporating remotely collected health information into the patient record or review workflow.

### Delivery Concept
The hackathon concept also explored how future healthcare delivery services, including medication delivery, could connect to the broader platform.

## Important Note

These features represent a hackathon prototype and product concept. They are not clinically validated medical tools.
